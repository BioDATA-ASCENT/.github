## Turn biological questions into working analyses

BioDATA ASCENT is an intensive, hands-on code-a-thon where early-career researchers use cloud computing, AI, and large-scale biological and biomedical data to develop working analyses, prototypes, reusable workflows, and visualizations.

Hosted immediately before the 2026 CSHL [Biological Data Science Conference](https://meetings.cshl.edu/meetings.aspx?meet=DATA&year=26), the event is designed especially for PhD students and postdoctoral researchers. Participants work in small teams with peers and mentors using NIH-supported datasets and cloud resources from the [Common Fund Data Ecosystem (CFDE)](https://commonfund.nih.gov/dataecosystem) and [NHGRI AnVIL](https://anvilproject.org/).

No fully formed project is required. Bring a scientific question, a technical skill, an early idea—or simply curiosity and a willingness to collaborate. Participants with a range of biological and computational backgrounds are welcome; no prior cloud-computing or AI experience is required.

## What participants will do

- Explore genomic, transcriptomic, spatial, and multi-omics datasets
- Develop AI-assisted and cloud-based approaches to biomedical research questions
- Form interdisciplinary teams and work alongside experienced mentors
- Build and document a tangible analysis, prototype, workflow, or visualization
- Present results and identify opportunities for continued collaboration

## Follow the project

Visit the [BioDATA ASCENT event website](https://biodata-ascent.github.io/) for the full schedule, available datasets, project ideas, registration information, and event updates. Code, workflows, documentation, and other resources from the code-a-thon will be shared across this GitHub organization.
